**Senior Software Architect & Engineering Team Lead** · Hyderabad, India · [iramesh.com](https://iramesh.com)

I've been building software for 13+ years and leading teams for 4+. Right now I own the architecture for a 17-repo green energy platform (solar, battery storage and EV charging) at **Electrum**, and I'm building **AASDLC**, an AI-augmented engineering lifecycle.

## About me

I work where backend systems meet real-world operations: solar fleets, battery storage, EV charging, and all the plumbing a real business runs on.

The hard problems usually aren't the algorithms. They're API contracts that still hold up ten years later, dashboards a field tech can read on a phone in direct sun, and deploys that don't wake anyone up at 3am. That's what I optimize for.

I've taken products from pilot to production. **PGO**, a PG & hostel platform I architected and built, shipped on Android and iOS, scaled to 350+ cities and went from a ₹20 lakh valuation to a ~₹2 crore sale. I've also taught myself a company's proprietary tooling from scratch when no manual existed yet: SJS at Synactive, which I used to turn legacy C++ SAP screens into single-page JSON apps.

These days I'm working on **AASDLC**, which lets a team hand production-safe implementation work to AI subagents. On the side I build voice AI and on-device LLMs, plus tools I use every day.
